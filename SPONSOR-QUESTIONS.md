# Account, Membership, Family Billing, and Booking Questions for Jeremy

Local-only working document. Do not commit or push.

## Membership lifecycle

1. Please confirm that members may cancel their own membership, cancellation takes effect immediately, already-issued invoices remain payable, and no refund is created automatically.
   - Provisional decision: immediate self-cancellation, existing invoices retained, no automatic refund.

2. Should six- and twelve-month memberships eventually expire automatically at the end of their term?
   - Provisional decision: no automatic fixed-term expiry.

3. Please confirm what information is required when a new guest account is created, and what additional information, if any, is required for a membership application. Are the current profile fields sufficient: name, phone, date of birth, emergency contact, consent records, fencing experience, goals, weapon, and preferred training schedule? If not, which fields should be added? Should approved members receive a membership number, and if so, what format and allocation rules apply?

4. What circumstances permit suspension, who may suspend a membership, and does suspension affect already-issued charges or bookings?

5. What refund rules apply to cancellation, suspension, rejected applications, or unused fixed-term membership periods?

6. How long must membership applications, review notes, lifecycle history, and associated personal information be retained?

## Dependents and guardians

7. How should dependents without personal email addresses create their required separate accounts?
   - Confirmed direction: a dependent must create their own CFA account first. A guardian can only request a link to an existing account, and the dependent must approve it.

8. Should guardians later be allowed to submit or manage a dependent's membership application?
   - Provisional decision: guardians cannot apply for dependents in v1.

9. Should a dependent support multiple guardians, and what are the approval, priority, and billing-transfer rules when billing responsibility changes?
   - Provisional decision: one active billing guardian per dependent in v1.

10. Exactly which dependent identity fields and booking details may an active guardian view? Please confirm whether date of birth, contact details, attendance, coach notes, and cancelled bookings are included.

11. Which notification channels should family-link requests, application decisions, changes requested, suspensions, and cancellations use: email, in-app, SMS, or a combination?

## Billing and records

12. When billing responsibility transfers, should new charges route from the acceptance time or from a billing-period boundary, and who handles charges already created in that period?

13. Are there any statutory, accounting, safeguarding, or club-policy retention requirements for family links, billing links, invoices, receipts, payment attempts, audit events, and private review notes?
